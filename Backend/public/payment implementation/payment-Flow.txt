6️⃣ Cancel Subscription (MOST CONFUSING PART) 👍🏼
Zoho / Industry standard

❌ Immediate cancel rarely hota hai
- user can use that plan after cancle until next billing date
Usually: cancel_at = cycle_end

Cancellation ke baad states:
Time	Status
Cancel click	cancelled
Billing end	expired
After grace	free

Permissions after CANCEL (before expiry)
Feature	Allowed

Every thing in plan
not plan upgrade ❌

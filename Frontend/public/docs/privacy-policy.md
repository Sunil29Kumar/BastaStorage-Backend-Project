# Privacy Policy 🛡️
**Last Updated: January 2026**

Welcome to **BastaStorage**. Your privacy is our top priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information and files.

---

### 1. Information We Collect 📂
We collect the following information to provide a seamless cloud storage experience:
* **Account Information:** Name, email address, and phone number.
* **Content:** Uploaded files, folder structures, and related metadata.
* **Usage Data:** Device information, IP addresses, and login activity logs.
* **Payment Identifiers:** Transaction IDs (Note: We do **not** store your card or bank details).

### 2. How We Use Your Data 👤
Your data is used strictly for:
* Providing and maintaining the BastaStorage service.
* Enhancing account security and authentication.
* Customer support and technical troubleshooting.
* Processing payments through secure gateways.

### 3. File Privacy & Ownership 🔐
* **Ownership:** You retain 100% ownership of any data you upload.
* **Access:** BastaStorage employees cannot view or access your private files unless required by law or specifically requested by you for support.

### 4. File Sharing 🔗
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Files shared via **Public Links** can be accessed by anyone who has the link. You are solely responsible for the links you generate and share.

### 5. Payments & Billing 💳
All payments are securely processed by **Razorpay**. BastaStorage does not have access to, nor do we store, your full credit card numbers or banking passwords.

### 6. Data Retention & Deletion 🗑️
* You can delete your files or account at any time.
* Once deleted, files are removed from our active servers. 
* There may be a grace period during which data remains in our encrypted backups before being permanently erased.

### 7. Cookies & Tracking 🍪
We use **Essential Cookies** only:
* We store a unique **Session ID** to keep you logged in.
* These cookies do not track you on other websites.
* Clearing your browser data will remove these session cookies.

### 8. Legal Compliance 🇮🇳
We comply with the applicable laws of **Bharat**. We may disclose your information if required to do so by a court order or other government legal process.

### 9. Security Measures 🔒
We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) and secure access controls to protect your data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
